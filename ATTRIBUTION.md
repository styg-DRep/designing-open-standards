# Attribution

## Adapted material

**Open Data Institute — Open Standards for Data guidebook**
<https://standards.theodi.org/> · © Open Data Institute · [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)

This framework adapts and builds on the guidebook, in particular:

- the four-phase structure of open standards development (Scope and Start, Development, Launch and Adoption, Review);
- the Open Standards for Data Canvas, adapted here as the *Simple Canvas* (note and canvas);
- the three families of standards — shared vocabularies, exchange, and guidance — adapted in *Types of Standards*, *Shared Vocabularies*, *Information Exchange* and *Provide Guidance*.

Changes: the material has been restructured as a node-and-edge map with checkpoints, phase gates and returns-to routes; re-grounded in decentralized and Cardano governance; and extended with new nodes, patterns, artifact templates and agent playbooks. Because the adapted material is CC BY-SA 4.0, this repository's framework content is released under the same license.
