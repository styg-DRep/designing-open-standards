## Intent

Provide the question set a review draws on — adoption, ease of adoption, use cases, impact, interoperability, errors, change requests, governance, sustainability, ecosystem position and scope integrity.

## Adoption

- Who is actually using the standard?
    
- Who stopped using it?
    
- Who never adopted it?
    
- Where is adoption concentrated?
    
- Has it become infrastructure?
    
- Is adoption deep or superficial?
    

---

## Ease of Adoption

- What costs do adopters face?
    
- Where do implementations stall?
    
- Which concepts create confusion?
    
- What support is repeatedly needed?
    
- Are workarounds common?
    

---

## Use Cases

- Which original use cases are active?
    
- Which are absent?
    
- Which new use cases emerged?
    
- Are adopters stretching the standard beyond its intended scope?
    

---

## Impact

- Did the intended change mechanisms occur?
    
- Did the standard improve the coordination condition identified in Scope?
    
- What evidence exists of impact?
    
- What unintended effects appeared?
    

---

## Interoperability

- Does the standard work with the standards and infrastructure it was expected to?
    
- Have incompatible profiles emerged?
    
- Are mappings stable?
    
- Have extensions fragmented interoperability?
    

---

## Errors

- What defects have been reported?
    
- Which recur?
    
- Which were not caught in Development testing?
    
- What does that reveal about the test model?
    

---

## Change Requests

- What changes are being requested?
    
- By whom?
    
- Which requests align with the original aims?
    
- Which represent legitimate scope expansion?
    
- Which would distort the standard away from its purpose?
    

---

## Governance

- Is the change process functioning?
    
- Are decisions timely?
    
- Are maintainers overloaded?
    
- Are objections handled credibly?
    
- Is authority too concentrated or too diffuse?
    
- Can new stewards enter?
    

---

## Sustainability

- What does maintenance actually require?
    
- Is funding adequate?
    
- Is support burden sustainable?
    
- Is documentation staying current?
    
- Are critical dependencies healthy?
    

---

## Ecosystem Position

- What new actors now depend on the standard?
    
- What power or control does the standard now mediate?
    
- Has it created new chokepoints?
    
- Has another standard displaced or absorbed its role?
    

---

## Scope Integrity

- Is the standard still solving the problem it was created to address?
    
- Has it become something else?
    
- Should the scope be narrowed, widened, or reframed?
    

---

# Pattern: Review the Theory of Change

A central Review question should be:

> **Was the causal theory broadly correct?**

Compare:

```text
assumed mechanism
vs
observed mechanism

assumed outcome
vs
observed outcome

assumed beneficiaries
vs
actual beneficiaries

assumed risks
vs
actual risks
```

If the Theory of Change no longer holds, the correct response may be to revisit Scope rather than merely edit the specification.
