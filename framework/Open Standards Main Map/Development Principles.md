## Intent

Define the commitments that should govern how the standard itself is developed.

The [OpenStand Principles](https://open-stand.org/about-us/principles/) are uniquely developed for Open Standards, but a Cardano-native process should express its own explicit procedural commitments rather than merely importing a named framework.

Likely commitments include:

- openness;
    
- transparency;
    
- accessible participation;
    
- collaborative development;
    
- documented decisions;
    
- due consideration of objections;
    
- public provenance;
    
- vendor neutrality;
    
- interoperability;
    
- ability to reuse and implement;
    
- transparent stewardship and change processes.
    

These commitments should later inform the governance model for the standard itself.
