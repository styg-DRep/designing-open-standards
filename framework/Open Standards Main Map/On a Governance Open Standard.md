## What is this effort building towards?

An open standard for governance should make it easier for individuals and groups to create, access, share, develop and implement better quality suggestions.

## What are standards?

Standards are documented, reusable agreements that solve a specific set of problems or meet clearly defined needs.

Standards detail the language, concepts, rules, guidance or results that have been agreed.

Standards are used when it’s important to be consistent, be able to repeat processes, make comparisons, or reach a shared understanding.

An ***open standard*** is a standard that’s available for anyone to access, use or share. They are open to the public and are developed and approved via a collaborative and consensus-driven process.

**Open standards for governance may be developed to:**

-   agree on a common model or a common language
-   share information consistently
-   make combining information simpler and more streamlined
-   support positive discussion
-   implement changes in the ecosystem
-   promote common understanding
