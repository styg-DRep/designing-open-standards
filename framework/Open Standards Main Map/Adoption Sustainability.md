The burden does not end with publication.

Adopters may need:

- implementation guidance;
    
- examples;
    
- validation;
    
- migration support;
    
- change notices;
    
- compatibility information;
    
- translations;
    
- troubleshooting.
    

Ask:

> **What ongoing work does adoption require from the adopter?**

A standard whose implementation burden exceeds its benefit will decay even if formally maintained.

---

# Pattern: Include the Adopter's Cost

Evaluate:

```text
benefit of interoperability
        versus
implementation cost
+ learning cost
+ migration cost
+ compliance cost
+ update cost
```

Do not optimize maintenance for the standards team by shifting unreasonable cost onto adopters.
