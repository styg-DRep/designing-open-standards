## Intent

Identify existing standards, conventions, specifications, schemas and common practices that already coordinate relevant parts of the ecosystem.

Do not restrict the search to things formally called standards.

Look for:

- formal specifications;
    
- CIP conventions;
    
- metadata schemas;
    
- APIs;
    
- governance procedures;
    
- templates;
    
- vocabularies;
    
- naming conventions;
    
- identifier systems;
    
- repositories;
    
- recurring community practices;
    
- implicit conventions embedded in widely used tools.
    

A de facto convention may constrain the ecosystem more strongly than a formally published standard.

## Working pattern: Standards archaeology

For each apparently shared practice ask:

1. Where did this convention come from?
    
2. Is it documented?
    
3. Who maintains it?
    
4. Who depends upon it?
    
5. Can it change?
    
6. Is participation in its development open?
    
7. What breaks if it changes?
