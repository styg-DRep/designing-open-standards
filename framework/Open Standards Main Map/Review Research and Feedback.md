## Intent

Ensure aims reflect what has been learned rather than merely what initiators originally intended.

Review:

- ecosystem research;
    
- existing standards;
    
- participant interviews;
    
- consultation;
    
- early adopter feedback;
    
- competing opportunity frames;
    
- technical discoveries;
    
- political or institutional constraints.
    

Ask:

> What have we learned that should change what we are trying to accomplish?

Especially preserve disagreements.

Aims should not imply consensus that does not exist.
