# Choose, Plan, and Approve the Development Method

## Intent

Choose and make explicit the way the standard will be developed, tested, changed, and prepared for release.

The purpose is not to select a fashionable project-management methodology.

The purpose is to establish a **development system appropriate to the uncertainty, participation requirements, risks, resources, and governance conditions of this particular standard.**

This cluster answers three progressively more concrete questions:

1. **How should this standard be developed?**
    
2. **What does that method require in practice?**
    
3. **Do the relevant participants have enough agreement, mandate, and capacity to begin?**

# Readiness Gate

Development is ready to begin when:

1. the aims and objectives are sufficiently clear;
    
2. the proposed method matches the current uncertainty;
    
3. meaningful contributors are available;
    
4. decision rights are explicit;
    
5. core development infrastructure exists;
    
6. testing is embedded in the plan;
    
7. contribution and licensing conditions are known;
    
8. major dependencies and interoperability requirements are visible;
    
9. realistic resources are available;
    
10. a plausible sustainability path exists;
    
11. the relevant approving or consenting actors have authorized progression;
    
12. outstanding objections are recorded.
    

---

# Possible Outcomes

## Approved to Develop

Proceed into Development.

## Approved Conditionally

Proceed with explicit conditions and review triggers.

## Revise Development Plan

The opportunity is sound but the method is inappropriate.

## Return to Aims

The development plan reveals unclear or conflicting objectives.

## Return to Ecosystem

Required actors, infrastructure, or dependencies are missing.

## Pause

The work is worthwhile but presently lacks resources, mandate, or sustainable capacity.

## Stop

The development effort no longer appears justified.

The important reciprocal edge is:

**Think About Sustainability —informs→ Development Plan**

and:

**Development Plan —returns-to→ Think About Sustainability**

because changes in development scope, method, infrastructure, or governance directly change the sustainability burden.
