## Intent

Assemble enough complementary capability to develop the standard without confusing participation with centralized ownership.

For a Cardano-native standard, organize around **functions that must be performed**, not titles that every project must fill.

## Pattern: Roles Are Functions, Not Offices

One person may hold several roles.

One role may be distributed among several people.

Roles may change as Development progresses.

The important thing is that necessary responsibilities are visible.
