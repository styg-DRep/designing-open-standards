## Intent

Develop a sufficiently grounded understanding of the environment in which a proposed governance standard would operate.

The purpose is not to create an exhaustive description of Cardano governance. It is to expose enough of the relevant system that the standards effort can understand:

- who is already acting;
    
- what they are doing;
    
- what infrastructure mediates those activities;
    
- how governance information moves;
    
- where relationships, dependencies and conflicts exist;
    
- what standards or conventions already structure activity;
    
- where influence and control appear to reside;
    
- who will be affected;
    
- who can participate meaningfully in development; and
    
- whether a new standard is actually required.
    

This node forms a counterpart to **Frame the Opportunity**.

**Frame the Opportunity asks:**

> What condition seems worth changing?

**Understand the Ecosystem asks:**

> What system produces, experiences and constrains that condition?

Neither should be considered reliable without the other.

---

# Gate condition

The ecosystem is understood well enough to proceed when the effort can provide a defensible provisional answer to:

1. Who performs the relevant governance activities?
    
2. Who uses the infrastructure through which those activities occur?
    
3. Who produces, consumes or exchanges relevant governance information?
    
4. What relationships connect these actors?
    
5. What technical, social and institutional infrastructure mediates their behavior?
    
6. What existing standards, conventions or protocols already apply?
    
7. Could an existing standard be used, extended or opened rather than creating another?
    
8. Where are important political, institutional or control tensions present?
    
9. Who is materially affected by the proposed change?
    
10. Who could help shape, test and advocate for the work?
    
11. What important parts of the ecosystem remain unknown?
    

Passing the gate does **not** mean the map is complete.

It means the map is good enough that proceeding would no longer depend primarily on an imagined ecosystem.

---

# Pattern: Map Before Designing

Do not begin by asking what the new standard should contain.

Begin by asking what people already do, what they already use, and what already coordinates their behavior.

Standards enter living systems. Even apparently technical standards alter relationships between actors, information and infrastructure.

A standards effort that cannot describe the system it proposes to intervene in is not yet ready to design the intervention.
