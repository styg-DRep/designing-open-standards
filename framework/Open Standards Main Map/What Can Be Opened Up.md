## Intent

Identify useful existing conventions or infrastructure whose primary limitation is not functionality but openness.

Consider whether something already works but is:

- proprietary;
    
- inaccessible;
    
- poorly documented;
    
- controlled by a narrow group;
    
- difficult to reuse;
    
- licensed restrictively;
    
- governed opaquely;
    
- unavailable in machine-readable form;
    
- technically open but practically inaccessible.
    

## Pattern: Distinguish invention from enclosure

Sometimes the ecosystem does not need a new standard.

It needs wider access to the standard it already effectively uses.
