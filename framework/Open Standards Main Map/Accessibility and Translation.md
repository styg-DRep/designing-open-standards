## Intent

Identify whether language, technical complexity, disability, tooling, culture, geography, bandwidth, or other access conditions could prevent intended participants from using or contributing to the standard.

Translation should be understood broadly.

It may include:

- spoken language;
    
- technical ↔ nontechnical language;
    
- human ↔ machine representation;
    
- expert ↔ newcomer communication;
    
- visual accessibility;
    
- low-bandwidth access;
    
- cultural or institutional context.
    

Ask:

> Who could theoretically use this standard but practically cannot?

This may materially change the objectives themselves.
