This node asks:

**Who creates, initiates, maintains, interprets, or implements the things that governance depends on?**

A “producer” is anyone whose activity creates a governance object, decision surface, institutional condition, or operational consequence that other participants then rely on.

Useful places to look include formal governance roles, proposal authors, working groups, standards authors, researchers, analysts, facilitators, tool builders, infrastructure maintainers, treasury applicants and administrators, delegates, reviewers, constitutional interpreters, implementation teams, moderators, registrars, indexers, and people maintaining persistent governance records.

The best way to identify them is to begin with **governance outputs rather than job titles**. Pick a visible object and ask who makes it exist.

For example:

- Who creates a governance proposal?
- Who produces the metadata that makes it discoverable?
- Who produces analysis or interpretation around it?
- Who convenes discussion?
- Who records decisions?
- Who turns a decision into implementation?
- Who maintains the systems that make those activities possible?

A useful working pattern is:

**Object → producing activity → actor**

So:

`proposal rationale → authorship → proposer`

`governance index → aggregation → indexer/tool maintainer`

`constitutional interpretation → evaluation → constitutional body / analysts`

This helps avoid a common mistake: mapping only formally authorized actors and missing the people who actually produce the information, interfaces, interpretations, and coordination that governance runs on.

#### Things to capture

For each producer, it is useful to record:

- what they produce;
- whether the output is formal or informal;
- whether production is continuous, episodic, or event-driven;
- who depends on the output;
- whether anyone else can perform the same role;
- what standards or conventions constrain the production;
- where the output becomes canonical, if anywhere.

A particularly useful question is:

> **What stops existing if this actor stops producing?**

That exposes hidden dependencies.
