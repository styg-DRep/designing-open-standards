# Launch and Adoption

## Intent

Move a sufficiently stable standard from controlled development into broader ecosystem use, while creating the support and observation needed to learn from adoption.

Launch and Adoption asks:

> **What happens when the standard becomes something other people actually depend upon?**

The phase has three linked purposes:

1. make the standard visible and practically adoptable;
    
2. support different kinds of adopters in using it;
    
3. observe how real adoption changes both the standard and the ecosystem around it.
    

Launch should therefore be understood as the beginning of broader learning, not the end of development.

---
# Persistent Return Edges

Launch and Adoption should remain able to return to:

### Documentation

when adopters cannot understand or implement the standard.

### Develop the Standard

when adoption reveals specification defects.

### Testing

when new real-world scenarios expose gaps.

### Sustainability

when support or maintenance burden becomes unexpectedly high.

### Understand the Ecosystem

when new actors, infrastructure, or control relationships emerge.

### Theory of Change

when adoption occurs but expected effects do not.

### Frame the Opportunity

in exceptional cases where real-world evidence undermines the original premise.

---

# Key Principle

**The ecosystem becomes the test environment.**

Development intentionally constructs test scenarios.

Launch reveals scenarios the development team did not imagine.

That is the core purpose of this phase.
