# Actor Map

> Stub. The Actor Map is an **artifact**, not an activity. It is produced by
> *Identify People and Groups* (supported by *Producers of Governance Activity*,
> *Users of Governance Infrastructure* and *Exchanging of Governance Information*),
> **informs** *Understand Relationships Between People and Groups* and **feeds**
> *Understand Governance Data and Infrastructure*.

From the Scope and Start gate, the Actor Map should make visible:

- who produces relevant governance activity;
- who uses relevant governance infrastructure;
- who exchanges or transforms governance information;
- recurring governance roles;
- actor gaps;
- infrastructure dependencies;
- likely relationship leads.

This provides the descriptive base from which stakeholder relevance and participation choices have been made.

Working pattern (from *Identify People and Groups*): **Actor → activity → governance object**.
