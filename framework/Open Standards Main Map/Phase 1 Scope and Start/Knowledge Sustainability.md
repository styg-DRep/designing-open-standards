A standard contains knowledge about why choices were made.

Preserve:

- rationale;
    
- rejected alternatives;
    
- historical decisions;
    
- examples;
    
- known limitations;
    
- implementation lessons;
    
- change history.
    

Without this context, later maintainers may accidentally recreate old failures or remove constraints whose purpose is no longer visible.

---

# Pattern: Preserve Why, Not Only What

A specification tells future participants what exists.

A decision record tells them why.

Both are needed for durable stewardship.
