Ask:

- Who supplies development effort?
    
- Who pays for infrastructure?
    
- Who funds maintenance?
    
- What work depends on volunteers?
    
- What happens when initial funding ends?
    
- Can maintenance effort decline as the standard stabilizes?
    

Distinguish:

**startup resources**

from

**ongoing resources**.

A project may have enough money to publish v1 but no credible capacity to maintain v1.1.

---

# Pattern: Price the Future, Not Just the Build

Estimate future obligations created by:

- every extension point;
    
- every supported format;
    
- every maintained tool;
    
- every translation;
    
- every validator;
    
- every compatibility guarantee;
    
- every governance process;
    
- every support promise.
    

Complexity is a recurring cost.
