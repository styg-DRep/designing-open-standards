## Intent

Identify what parts of the standard need to remain stable and what parts should permit adaptation.

Ask:

- What must remain consistent ecosystem-wide?
    
- What should local communities be able to extend?
    
- What variation is harmless?
    
- What variation destroys interoperability?
    
- How might new governance practices emerge?
    
- How should unknown future use cases be accommodated?
    

## Pattern: Preserve a Stable Core and an Open Edge

A healthy standard often needs both:

**stable core**

Enough consistency to enable coordination.

**open edge**

Enough adaptability to allow learning, domain variation, and future development.

Do not confuse extensibility with unrestricted variation.
