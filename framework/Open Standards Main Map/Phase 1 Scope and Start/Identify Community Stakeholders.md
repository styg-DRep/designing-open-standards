## Intent

Transform the broad ecosystem map into a more focused understanding of the people whose participation matters to this specific standards effort.

This is an act of **curation**, not merely classification.

The question changes from:

> Who exists in this ecosystem?

to:

> Whose knowledge, interests, capabilities or exposure to consequences make their participation important here?

Two inquiries feed this process:
- Classification of Ecosystem Tensions
- Selection of Ecosystem Stakeholders 

# Pattern: Representation Is Not Participation

Identifying a stakeholder does not mean that stakeholder has been consulted.

Consulting one member of a community does not mean the community has been represented.

Inviting participation does not mean meaningful participation was possible.

Record these states separately.

Possible participation states might include:

`identified → contacted → engaged → contributing → testing → stewarding`

This makes gaps visible instead of hiding them behind a stakeholder list.
