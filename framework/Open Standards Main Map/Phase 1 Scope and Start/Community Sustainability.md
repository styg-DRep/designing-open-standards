A standard persists because people continue to understand, use, improve, and care about it.

Ask:

- Are contributors replaceable?
    
- Is knowledge shared?
    
- Can newcomers understand the work?
    
- Are decisions documented?
    
- Is contribution rewarding enough to continue?
    
- Is participation concentrated in one institution?
    
- Are maintainers burning out?
    

---

# Pattern: Avoid Heroic Maintenance

If continued operation depends upon:

> one person who understands everything,

the standard is not sustainable.

Create:

- documented decisions;
    
- onboarding paths;
    
- contributor guides;
    
- shared ownership of critical knowledge;
    
- predictable maintenance routines.
