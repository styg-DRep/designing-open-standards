# Route: Phase 3 — Launch and Adoption

Notes are in `framework/Open Standards Main Map/Phase 3 Launch and Adoption/`. Baseline: this cycle's Development Outcomes Bundle.

This phase runs for as long as adoption takes. Guided sessions here are usually periodic check-ins rather than one sitting. "Continue adoption monitoring" is a normal gate outcome, not a failure. The ecosystem is now the test environment.

| # | Node(s) to read | Write to | Exit check |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Launch and Adoption | `effort.yaml` (`phase: launch`, Development baseline recorded) | They know the three purposes (adoptable, supported, observed) and the persistent return edges |
| 1 | Plan and Launch | `launch.yaml` → `launch` | The launch answers the questions in its Intent; the capability is launched, not just the artifact; the stable boundary is visible; each audience has a next action |
| 2 | Support Adoption | `launch.yaml` → `support_issues` | Support exists for each stage of the adoption journey; requests are classified and fed back as evidence |
| 3 | Monitor Adoption | `launch.yaml` → `adopters`, `snapshots` | Depth, not reach; indirect use looked for; forks read as feedback; adoption success separated from impact success; Scope artifacts snapshotted, not overwritten |
| 4 | Launch and Adoption Outcomes (Phase Gate) | `launch-and-adoption-outcomes.yaml`, draft `review_readiness_record` | Sufficiency across the gate criteria (`toolkit/launch-gate-criteria.yaml`); which trigger applies; the review questions adoption has produced |

Prompts worth reaching for: "what position has the standard actually taken in the ecosystem?", "who is affected without knowing the standard by name?", "is this divergence misuse or a missing extension point?", "what has happened that we did not expect?".

Use the persistent return edges when adoption exposes defects (Develop the Standard, Testing, Documentation, sustainability, ecosystem, theory of change, and in exceptional cases Frame the Opportunity). Entering review moves to **Review**, normally via **Deciding To Review**.
