Prompts worth reaching for: "if we removed the word *standard*, would this still describe a problem?", "who can say no, who can leave?", "what stops existing if this actor stops producing?", "interoperable with what, for what purpose?", "who maintains this?", "who becomes less legible if this succeeds?".

At the gate the effort inherits obligations (participation, testing, openness, interoperability, inclusivity, stewardship, licensing, documentation, review). Record the bundle as the cycle's Scope baseline in `effort.yaml`. Entering development moves to **Develop the Standard**.
